About me

Hi, I'm Satwik. I point cameras at comedians, poets, mountains and the occasional flamingo, then cut it all into stories you can't scroll past.

Half cinematographer, half editor, fully obsessed with the half-second where a moment turns into a memory. You'll find me side-stage at stand-up shows, wandering Ahmedabad's old stone streets, or deep in a timeline at 2 a.m., re-cutting a reel until it feels just right.

Before this I planned media at Dentsu, so I know exactly why your thumb stops scrolling. These days I'm at MICA, learning the science behind the stories, and still looking up at the stars every chance I get.
